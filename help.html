Help & User Guide

Follow these steps:

  1. Collect your soil data (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, pH).
  2. Enter weather information (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall).
  3. Enter crop type and region in the “Enter Data” page.
  4. Click “Predict Yield” to see your result.

For assistance, contact your nearest agriculture department or email support@cropyieldpredictor.com.
