About Crop Yield Prediction

What We Use
  • Soil Nutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K)
  • Soil pH Level
  • Weather Data: Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall
  • Crop Type and Region
Why These Matter
  • NPK determines soil fertility and plant health.
  • Temperature affects growth rate and maturity.
  • Rainfall & Humidity influence water availability and disease resistance.
  • pH impacts nutrient absorption capacity.
